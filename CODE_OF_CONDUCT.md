# Code of Conduct

We want EnvDoctor to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity or location. This code is inspired by the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and assume good intent; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.
- Respect other people's privacy and time.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or advocating any of the above.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who violates this code.
To report a problem, use the repository's private **Security → Report a vulnerability** form (mention "Code of Conduct"), or contact a maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies in all project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
